Privacy policy

Geron Accounting Bot. Effective 22 October 2026.

Geron Accounting Bot is a private Telegram bot run by one person, its owner, for chats the owner manages. This policy explains what data it handles and why.

Telegram data

The bot acts only on commands addressed to it. For each command it keeps the message, the sender's Telegram ID and name, and the chat's ID and title, so that it can process the command exactly once and reply. Other messages aren't kept. This record is stored on the owner's computer.

Ledger data

Entries recorded through the bot are written, with their amount, currency, note, time and the Telegram ID of whoever recorded them, to a Google Sheets ledger owned by the owner. A change to an entry or a rate is also logged with the name of the person who made it. Only the owner and the bot can open a ledger, unless the owner shares it with someone.

Google user data

The owner authorizes the bot once with the Google Drive permission drive.file. With it, the bot:

  • creates spreadsheets in the owner's Drive, in a folder it made, named after a chat;
  • finds those spreadsheets again, using a tag it put on them;
  • shares each one with the bot's own Google service account, so that it can keep the ledger.

This permission covers only files the bot creates. The bot can't see or open any other file in the owner's Drive, and it doesn't access Gmail, contacts or any other Google data. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.

The bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Data isn't sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone. It stays in Telegram, in the owner's Google account and on the owner's computer. There are no analytics or trackers, and these pages set no cookies.

Retention and removal

Ledgers stay in the owner's Drive until the owner deletes them. The owner can withdraw the bot's Google access at any time under Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps and services. Removing the bot from a Telegram chat stops it from receiving that chat's commands.

Changes

If the bot's use of data changes, this page will be updated and its effective date changed.
